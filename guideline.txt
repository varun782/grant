Community Green Tech Innovation Grant 2026 - Official Guidelines

Overview:
The Community Green Tech Innovation Grant provides funding to local organizations leveraging technology to solve environmental issues.

Mandatory Eligibility Requirements:
1. The applicant must be a legally registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.
2. The organization must have been in continuous operation for a minimum of 2 years prior to application.
3. The applicant's total organizational operating budget for the previous fiscal year must not exceed $500,000.
4. The application must include an implementation timeline not exceeding 12 months.

Required Documentation (Attachments):
- A detailed itemized project budget (Attachment A).
- A copy of the organization's most recent tax return (Form 990).

Recommended Submissions (Bonus points):
- We highly recommend applicants partner with a local public educational institution.
- Projects that commit to releasing their software under an open-source license will be prioritized.
